Data and code availability. The complete implementation of CROSS-MORSE (frozen plants, transport adapter, action registry, sensing, observer, consequence models, the nine policy families, selection, confirmation and admission statistics, and the sealed eight-stage lifecycle), the dataset adapters with one entry point and three configurations per dataset, the Slurm reproduction launcher with the Arrhenius site settings, the environment specifications, the test suite, the builders of the run-derived tables and figures, the code of the experiments outside the eight-stage run (the budget-matched arms E3–E6, the pinned-upstream driver of the retrained external baselines, the E17 timing protocol and the scheduler-accounting capture) with the exporter that turns their outputs into traceable measurement records, and a registry of every value reported in this article are available at https://github.com/VKHS/CR-9. All seven datasets are official public releases obtained from their providers through the repository's adapters; the split rule, seeds, fold structure and margins of every configuration are registered in the repository. The launcher (run_cross_morse.sh reproduce) regenerates the benchmark results on a Slurm GPU cluster: it checks the source identity of the released runtime, which every run record seals, executes the eight-stage lifecycle on every dataset (Roman-empire on its ten official splits), extracts the role membership of D_fit, D_explore, D_select, D_confirm, D_compare, D_admit and D_test into each run's manifest, rebuilds the run-derived tables and figures of this article from the regenerated runs and places each regenerated value beside the printed one. Two complete synthetic example runs verify the software workflow without benchmark data and are kept apart from the benchmark tables.
