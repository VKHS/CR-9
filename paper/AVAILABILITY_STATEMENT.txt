Code and data. The CROSS-MORSE reference implementation, seven dataset adapters, configurations, environment specifications, tests, experiment drivers and reconstruction tools are available at https://github.com/VKHS/CR-9. Official datasets are obtained from their providers through the adapters; the repository specifies the split rules and seeds. The run index registers 0 of 7 official full-profile dataset archives, and the record index registers 0 of 10 auxiliary measurement-record sets. No official run archive is registered for ogbn-arxiv, ogbn-proteins, ogbn-products, Roman-empire, PascalVOC-SP, COCO-SP, Peptides-func. With the required validated archives, make paper reconstructs the run-derived columns of main Tables 1–2 and Supplementary Tables S4, S12, S12c, S13–S15, S23 and S41; numerical plots related to main Figs. 2c and 4d and Supplementary Fig. S9; a partial internal-method map related to main Fig. 5a; an entry-loss depth diagnostic; and stage-cost bars related to Table S41. Auxiliary record-table builders and groups without a dedicated display builder are identified in paper/CLAIMS.md. make review checks registered public run deposits against their pinned file hashes, rebuilds the supported displays and runs the scientific evidence audit for its enumerated core results and auxiliary records. Index flags and DOI references alone do not establish public availability or agreement with the manuscript; the review report records the checks and remaining limits. Bundled synthetic examples demonstrate the software workflow and are not benchmark measurements.
