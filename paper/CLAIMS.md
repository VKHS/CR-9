# Claim-to-evidence map

Generated from `paper/sources.json`, `records/specs.json` and the run/record indexes. `make claims-check` checks freshness. This map covers the registered manuscript groups, including those without a dedicated display builder.

## Results in this release

- This is a code release: run outputs are regenerated with `bash run_cross_morse.sh reproduce` ([guide](../docs/HPC.md)); its report rebuilds the run-derived displays below from the regenerated runs and places every compared quantity beside the printed value.
- Record sets of the experiments outside the lifecycle are regenerated by their drivers ([Experiments](../docs/EXPERIMENTS.md)) and traced to their raw files by `make records-check`.

Analytical calculations use registered formulas and inputs. Registry plots remain transcriptions or analytical scenarios rather than replays of benchmark observations.
