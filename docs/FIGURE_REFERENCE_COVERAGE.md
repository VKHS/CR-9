# Figure reference coverage

The display inventory holds **25 quantitative figures**. **Fourteen have dedicated full-figure reference contracts:** main Figure 5 and supplementary Figures S1, S5, S6, S7, S8, S9, S10, S11, S12, S13, S15, S19 and S20. The other **eleven** (main Figures 2, 3, 4 and 6; supplementary Figures S2–S4, S14, S16, S17 and S18) are compared through their related tables; a full contract for them takes the original plotted data or a scenario declaration that the printed tables and formulas do not carry. Main Figure 1 is an illustration.

## Sources and interpretation

The source identities, hashes and evidence classifications are recorded in [paper/sources.json](../paper/sources.json). Figure identities, exact pages, related tables and implementation links are recorded in [paper/display_manifest.json](../paper/display_manifest.json). **All pages below are one-based PDF pages; printed page numbers agree for these source files.** “Main” means the registered main manuscript; “SI” means the registered supplementary information. The rendered figures were inspected alongside their captions and supporting tables, including the additional method labels in Figure S4.

The [source transcription record](../paper/transcription_check.json), [reported table values](../paper/reported_results.json), [additional printed display values](../paper/printed_display_values.json) and [registered constants](../paper/registered_constants.json) serve different roles. A generator computes from supplied run/record artifacts and declared analytical inputs; independent reference values are comparison targets. The [display framework](../paper/build/displays/framework.py) counts a figure as fully covered only through its own contract.

Figure support is of three kinds:

| Kind | Meaning | What a full contract takes |
|---|---|---|
| **Reference work** | Published tables, captions or explicit formulas appear sufficient to specify the figure's quantitative targets. | An independently enumerated target map covering every relevant panel, series, endpoint, interval and annotation, with source provenance and precision. Empirical recomputation reads the experiment evidence. |
| **Original plotted data** | Exact plotted quantities are not supplied by the published numerical tables or explicit analytical inputs. | The original arrays, records or source export, with their study identity and provenance. Unlabelled image coordinates are not exact source data. |
| **Scenario clarification** | Relevant values or formulas exist, but the plotted population, analytical setting, nominal baseline or measured/reference role is not sufficiently specified. | A source-backed declaration or original plot/study configuration. Settings must not be inferred solely to force agreement with the figure. |
