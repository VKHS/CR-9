# Figure reference coverage and remaining plan

**Assessment date: 8 November 2026. Status: AI-assisted, source-bound implementation assessment.**

This document records completed independent figure-reference contracts and the remaining work against the supplied manuscript and supplementary information. Completing a contract specifies what reconstruction must compare; it does not supply new measurements, replace original experiment records or certify that the original figure has been reproduced.

The inventory contains **25 quantitative figures**. **Six now have dedicated full-figure reference contracts:** supplementary Figures S1, S5, S8, S10, S11 and S19. **Nineteen still require contracts:** main Figures 2–6 and supplementary Figures S2–S4, S6–S7, S9, S12–S18 and S20. Main Figure 1 is an illustration. A missing figure contract does not by itself establish that an experiment is missing: many remaining targets can be specified from published tables and explicit analytical inputs. Conversely, a completed contract does not establish that the original measured inputs are available or agree with it.

## Sources and interpretation

The source identities, hashes and evidence classifications are recorded in [paper/sources.json](../paper/sources.json). Figure identities, exact pages, related tables and implementation links are recorded in [paper/display_manifest.json](../paper/display_manifest.json). **All pages below are one-based PDF pages; printed page numbers agree for these source files.** “Main” means the registered main manuscript; “SI” means the registered supplementary information. The rendered figures were inspected alongside their captions and supporting tables, including the additional method labels in Figure S4.

The [source transcription record](../paper/transcription_check.json), [reported table values](../paper/reported_results.json), [additional printed display values](../paper/printed_display_values.json), and [registered constants](../paper/registered_constants.json) serve different roles. A generator must compute from supplied run/record artifacts and declared analytical inputs. Independent reference values are comparison targets, not substitute experiment outputs. The [display framework](../paper/build/displays/framework.py) therefore does not treat a few checks against related tables as full coverage of a figure.

This assessment distinguishes three kinds of remaining work:

| Kind | Meaning | What closes it |
|---|---|---|
| **Reference work** | Published tables, captions or explicit formulas appear sufficient to specify the figure's quantitative targets. | An independently enumerated target map covering every relevant panel, series, endpoint, interval and annotation, with source provenance and precision. The underlying experiment evidence is still needed for empirical recomputation. |
| **Original plotted data** | Exact plotted quantities are not supplied by the published numerical tables or explicit analytical inputs. | The original arrays, records or source export, with their study identity and provenance. Unlabelled image coordinates are not exact source data. |
| **Scenario clarification** | Relevant values or formulas exist, but the plotted population, analytical setting, nominal baseline or measured/reference role is not sufficiently specified. | A source-backed declaration or original plot/study configuration. Settings must not be inferred solely to force agreement with the figure. |

Some figures need more than one kind of work. The classifications below describe available source support and the next implementation step; they do not change the repository's completeness checks.

For analytical curves, the shared convention should make the mathematical object independently reviewable: source-backed parameters and formulas; the required family of series; axis domains and units; named operating points; uncertainty/scenario meanings; and precision justified by the printed inputs. Every generated coordinate should be checked against that specification, with checks that detect a missing series or omitted domain. Renderer-selected sample spacing can be documented as an implementation choice. Reference arrays must not be generated from the current computed outputs and then used to certify those same outputs.

For empirical curves, formulas, smooth fits or image digitization cannot replace absent observations. The most useful original-data batches are runtime scaling; mechanism and decision-origin records; depth diagnostics; feedback/comparator ensemble grids; reliability and critic audits; and the three S4-only architecture pairs with their study-scope clarification. Main Figure 6 additionally needs its separate decoding-cost scenario. Figure S18 needs its analytical baseline/grid declaration.

## Completion and review

A full-figure coverage claim requires every relevant panel, series, coordinate family, interval and numerical annotation to be accounted for by an independent contract. A reproduction success additionally requires the underlying artifacts and computed quantities to pass their evidence and source-precision checks. Newly executed results, printed reference values, analytical scenarios and synthetic software fixtures must remain distinguishable throughout that process.

Before marking an item complete, review its source pages, target provenance, data roles and coverage against the [figure manifest](../paper/display_manifest.json) and the [printed-value guidance](PAPER_REFERENCE_VALUES.md). Update this plan and the [availability review](AVAILABILITY_REVIEW.md) from the resulting evidence. This AI-assisted assessment identifies bounded next steps; it does not replace source-author clarification where the original plot or study settings remain unspecified.
