The printed manuscript text places the run archives and measurement records at deposit DOIs listed in `runs/index.json` and `records/index.json`, whereas the registered statement and the publication workflow place them in the repository's release. Two routes close the claims that depend on this:

- **Route R (release).** Describe the release as the location of the run archives and measurement records in the printed paragraphs, as the registered statement already does; `make paper`, `make records-tables`, `make tables RUNS_ROOT=$ARTIFACTS_ROOT/runs` and `make figures` then operate on the published, verified assets, and every printed claim is fulfilled at publication.
- **Route Z (Zenodo).** Keep the printed wording, additionally deposit every run archive through the evidence-registration workflow (`make evidence-deposit`, `evidence-publish PUBLISH=1`, `evidence-register`; [Evidence registration](EVIDENCE_REGISTRATION.md)) and add record-set deposit registration to the record contract. The run archives whose complete tier exceeds one Zenodo record (section C) need the analysis tier on Zenodo with the complete tier in the release, or a quota agreed with Zenodo.

`make availability-fulfilled` is the final gate for both texts: the registered statement and every printed claim.
